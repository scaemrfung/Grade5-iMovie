Assignments

Four student projects

Each project includes planning, a step-by-step walkthrough, and a 1–4 assessment plan.

Project 1 · Due after Lessons 1–5 · 30–60 seconds

All About Me

A personal introduction using mostly MacBook-camera clips, basic editing, titles, and transitions.

Audience: Classmates and family. Keep it friendly, true, and easy to follow.

Must include

  • 4–7 short clips or photos
  • Opening title with the student’s name
  • 2–3 purposeful transitions
  • Clear beginning, middle, and end

Plan first

  1. 1Write three beats: Who I am · One thing I love · One thing I want people to know.
  2. 2List shots: face intro, object or hobby, optional photo, closing smile.
  3. 3Keep each recorded shot under 10 seconds.

Walkthrough

  1. 1Create New → Movie. Name FirstName_AllAboutMe.
  2. 2Record 3 short talking clips with FaceTime HD Camera. Import extras if wanted.
  3. 3Arrange intro · details · ending. Trim shaky starts. Target 30–60 seconds.
  4. 4Add opening title, optional lower third, end text. Add 2–3 transitions.
  5. 5Watch once without pausing. Fix spelling, timing, and messy cuts.
  6. 6Export: Share → File → 1080p → FirstName_AllAboutMe.mp4.

Assessment (1–4 each · total /16 · proficient ≥12)

Proficient bar. 30–60 seconds, named correctly, a classmate can follow who the student is.

1 Beginning — Story

Clips feel random. No clear beginning or ending.

2 Developing — Story

There is an intro, but the middle jumps or the ending cuts off.

3 Proficient — Story

Beginning, middle, and end are clear. Length is in range.

4 Extending — Story

Three beats feel planned and personal. Ending lands on purpose.

1 — Editing

Shaky starts, long pauses, leftover mistakes.

2 — Editing

Some trims done; several clips still messy.

3 — Editing

Clips trimmed. 2–3 transitions used with care.

4 — Editing

Tight pacing. Transitions help, never decorate every cut.

Student self-check

  • Name on opening title?
  • Partner can read every title?
  • Ums and shaky starts cut?
  • 30–60 seconds and 1080p export?

Project 2 · Due after Lessons 6–9 · 45–90 seconds

How-To or A Day in My Life

Instructional or narrative piece with voiceover or on-camera speaking and balanced music.

Must include

  • Voiceover or on-camera speaking on the MacBook
  • Background music at a proper level
  • Titles and transitions
  • At least one sound effect

Plan first

  1. 1Choose How-To (4–7 steps) or A Day in My Life (morning → school → highlight → end).
  2. 2Write short commands and matching narration for each step.
  3. 3Mark where one sound effect will land.

Walkthrough

  1. 1Create Movie named FirstName_HowTo or FirstName_DayInMyLife.
  2. 2One clip per step. Trim hard. Target 45–90 seconds.
  3. 3Record voiceover. Re-record muddy takes. Voice louder than music.
  4. 4Add iMovie music (ducked), one sound effect, titles, 2–4 transitions.
  5. 5Optional Ken Burns on stills. Export 1080p with a clear file name.

Assessment (1–4 each · total /20 · proficient ≥15)

Proficient bar. A first-time viewer can follow the steps or day; voice is louder than music.

Sequence

1 confusing → 4 tight, complete, easy to teach back

Voice

1 missing/unclear → 4 paced, clear, matches picture

Music & SFX

1 overpowering → 4 balanced, faded, planned effect

Titles & edit

1 little editing → 4 required titles and clean trims

Project 3 · Due after Lessons 10–12 · 60–90 seconds

Mini Story or Trailer-Style Piece

Creative or informative video showing pacing control and visual interest.

Must include

  • Varied pacing (normal + speed change or freeze)
  • Titles, music or voice, transitions
  • Ken Burns, purposeful effect, or Trailer structure

Walkthrough

  1. 1Pick Mini Story (Movie) or Trailer template. Plan Hook, Middle, Ending.
  2. 2Add one Slow/Fast or Freeze Frame — or complete every Trailer box with original media.
  3. 3Replace all template placeholders. Titles and audio cleaned up.
  4. 4Export 1080p as FirstName_Story.mp4 or FirstName_Trailer.mp4.

Assessment (1–4 each · total /20 · proficient ≥15)

Proficient bar. Hook, middle, ending, plus one clear pacing choice (or complete trailer).

Structure

1 no hook → 4 strong first 5 seconds and chosen last shot

Pacing

1 one speed only → 4 purposeful speed/freeze/trailer craft

Audio

1 missing/overpowering → 4 clean mix supporting the picture

Titles

1 placeholders left → 4 original, readable titles

Project 4 · Due at end of unit · 60–90 seconds

Final Showcase

Student choice: short documentary, original story, positive-habit commercial, or expanded trailer. Polished and ready for YouTube or Instagram.

Must include

  • Most unit skills (picture, titles, audio, one advanced choice)
  • Clean 1080p export with a clear file name
  • Optional pairs with defined roles

Walkthrough

  1. 1Create FirstName_Showcase. Rough cut: hook → middle → ending.
  2. 2Add titles, audio, and one advanced skill (Ken Burns, speed/freeze, Trailer, or PiP).
  3. 3Cold watch on headphones. Fix spelling, volume, privacy, last frame.
  4. 4Peer review (two stars + one wish). Export 1080p. Write 3-line reflection.

Assessment (1–4 each · total /20 · proficient ≥15)

Proficient bar. Polished 1080p file a stranger could follow, plus reflection.

Story

1 unclear purpose → 4 audience knows why it exists in 5 seconds

Editing

1 rough → 4 tight, varied, controlled

Audio & titles

1 unreadable/inaudible → 4 designed together; voice wins

Export

1 no file → 4 clean 1080p, clear name, social-ready
